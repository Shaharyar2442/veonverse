# Principle 10: We Never Give Up

## Core Definition & Summary
We Never Give Up means demonstrating unyielding grit, emotional composure, and resilience when encountering major strategic roadblocks. Leaders treat adversity as a learning mechanism to re-strategize, adapt, and drive forward to completion.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Grit vs. Sunk-Cost Containment.
- The Forced Choice: When a major strategic rollout hits massive structural barriers, a traditional pragmatist will cut losses, abandon the initiative, and pivot to something easier. This tension evaluates if a candidate treats a major roadblock as a definitive stop-sign or as a data-gathering lesson to re-strategize, adapt, and push forward anyway.

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Overcoming Obstacles (Pursues goals despite discouragement/opposition), Handling Stress (Manages pressure well, recovers quickly from setbacks).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Intrapersonal / Interpersonal & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Transform setbacks into tactical learning opportunities.
- Maintain calm under high stress and keep teams focused on long-term goals.
- Persist relentlessly until strategic objectives are realized.
