# Principle 4: We Put Results Above Rituals

## Core Definition & Summary
We Put Results Above Rituals means prioritizing tangible business outcomes and customer value over bureaucratic protocol, unnecessary committee approvals, and rigid governance rituals.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Outcome-Driven Agility vs. Protocol Compliance.
- The Forced Choice: This tests a candidate's comfort with missing structural alignment. When speed-to-market is critical, does the candidate halt progress to wait for formal governance committees and multiple sign-offs (Protocol), or do they accept accountability, ship a compliant but lean alternative, and update stakeholders afterward (Agility)?

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Driving Performance (Accomplishes goals and achieves results), Accountability (Accepts responsibility for actions regardless of outcomes).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Leadership / Leadership & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Eliminate red tape and streamline governance to accelerate delivery.
- Take personal ownership of outcomes rather than hiding behind compliance processes.
- Focus effort on impactful customer results rather than vanity metrics or meeting rituals.
