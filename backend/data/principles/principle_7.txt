# Principle 7: We Aim for Audacious Impact

## Core Definition & Summary
We Aim for Audacious Impact means setting game-changing, exponential goals that reshape markets rather than settling for incremental, safe progress. Leaders champion bold vision, strategic disruption, and relentless execution.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Exponential Moonshots (Disruption) vs. Incremental Security (Safe Margins).
- The Forced Choice: This tests the candidate's personal risk profile. Given a choice between a project with a 95% chance of delivering a modest 3% growth (Safe Incrementalism) versus a strategic initiative with a 40% chance of fundamentally transforming the market landscape (Audacious Impact), which path do they champion?

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Driving Strategy (Directs effort to achieve long-term objectives), Overcoming Obstacles (Pursues goals despite discouragement/opposition).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Leadership / Leadership & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Think at exponential scale rather than linear increments.
- Align team resources around high-leverage strategic moonshots.
- Persevere through initial setbacks to achieve transformational results.
