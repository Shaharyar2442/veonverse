# Principle 8: We Incentivize with Integrity

## Core Definition & Summary
We Incentivize with Integrity means uncompromising adherence to ethical boundaries and values. No commercial goal, financial incentive, or aggressive target is worth compromising integrity, compliance, or stakeholder trust.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Value Driven Boundary vs. Results at All Costs.
- The Forced Choice: This is a vital baseline check. The tension must place a high-stakes, massive financial or commercial target on the line, but create a scenario where hitting that target requires operating in an ambiguous, unprincipled grey area. It separates individuals who compromise values to secure an outcome from those who protect integrity first.

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Integrity (Acts honestly in accordance with moral/ethical principles), Accountability (Accepts responsibility for actions regardless of outcomes).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Intrapersonal / Interpersonal & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Establish unshakeable ethical guardrails for all commercial operations.
- Reject shortcuts or grey-area practices even under high revenue pressure.
- Reward authentic integrity and hold teams accountable to high compliance standards.
