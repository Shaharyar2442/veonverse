# Principle 6: Courage Fuels Our Leadership

## Core Definition & Summary
Courage Fuels Our Leadership means practicing radical candor, intellectual honesty, and standing up for what is right even when socially or politically uncomfortable. True leaders challenge flawed assumptions respectfully to protect the company's objective success.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Radical Candor & Intellectual Honesty vs. Social Preservation & Diplomatic Harmony.
- The Forced Choice: In many corporate ecosystems, politeness, preserving peer relationships, and deferring to authority are seen as essential political survival skills. This tension measures if a candidate will stay quiet during a meeting to maintain corporate harmony, or politely but firmly challenge a flawed executive assumption to protect the company's objective success.

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Taking Smart Risks (Evaluates trade-offs and acts accordingly), Integrity (Acts honestly in accordance with moral/ethical principles).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Intrapersonal / Interpersonal & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Speak truth to power with respect, transparency, and data.
- Address hard truths immediately rather than letting issues fester for harmony's sake.
- Stand up for ethical boundaries and long-term organizational health.
