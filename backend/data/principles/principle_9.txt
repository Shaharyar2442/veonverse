# Principle 9: We Stand Strong Together

## Core Definition & Summary
We Stand Strong Together means fostering an interdependent ecosystem over lone-wolf behaviors. True leaders build cross-functional synergy, elevate team capabilities, and win together as one unified organization.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Interdependent Ecosystem vs. Lone-Wolf.
- The Forced Choice: Many aggressive cultures reward the siloed top performer who crushes their numbers alone, regardless of the wake they leave behind. This scenario evaluates whether a candidate will slow down their personal sprint to ensure cross-functional alignment and lift their team's collective capacity, or simply look out for their own target.

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Teamwork (Collaborates with others to achieve goals), Relationship Building (Develops collaborative relationships for objectives).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Interpersonal / Interpersonal & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Break down departmental silos and share resources across functions.
- Prioritize collective victory and cross-functional success over individual accolades.
- Support colleagues during high-pressure cycles to ensure resilience across the ecosystem.
