# Principle 5: We Hire for Potential and Drive

## Core Definition & Summary
We Hire for Potential and Drive means choosing high-learning-agility, ambitious talent capable of exponential growth over predictable candidates with static linear experience. Leaders mentor, empower, and build capabilities for the future.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: High Potential Drivers vs. Safe Bets with Tested Experience.
- The Forced Choice: When hiring or building a team, standard managers look for candidates with linear backgrounds who have performed the exact same task for a decade (low risk). This tension forces them to choose between hiring a safe, predictable expert versus an unproven, high potential candidate with massive learning agility.

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Developing People (Provides support, coaching, training, career direction), Self-development (Actively acquires new knowledge and skills).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Leadership / Leadership & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Evaluate candidates based on grit, problem-solving capacity, and curiosity.
- Invest in coaching and accelerating high-potential talent.
- Avoid over-relying on traditional tenure or credentials.
