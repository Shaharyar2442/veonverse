# Principle 3: We Fight Against Mediocrity

## Core Definition & Summary
We Fight Against Mediocrity means rejecting "good enough" outcomes and actively raising standards through ownership, rigor, and continuous improvement. True leaders exhibit constructive dissatisfaction with the status quo to elevate performance from good to world-class.

## Psychometric Tension & Forced Choice Dilemma
- Psychometric Tension: Constructive Dissatisfaction (Restlessness) vs. Contented Steady-State Execution (Stability).
- The Forced Choice: Stable environments value employees who say, "This process is working smoothly, hits our baseline targets, and keeps the team calm—let's leave it alone." This principle tests whether a candidate is willing to intentionally disrupt an active, functional routine because they spot a way to optimize it from good to world-class.

## Hogan Competency Alignment Mapping
- Core Hogan Competency Targets: Driving for Results (Accomplishes goals, completes tasks, achieves results), Self-development (Actively acquires new knowledge and skills).
- Associated Behavioral Domains: Intrapersonal / Interpersonal & Interpersonal.

## Coaching & Key Behaviors
- Continuously raise the quality bar even when existing metrics meet minimum standards.
- Give candid, high-standard feedback to eliminate complacency.
- Foster personal mastery and continuous learning across teams.
